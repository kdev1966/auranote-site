Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

AuraNote is a note-taking app for Android, developed by Kaabaoui Othman. It sorts your notes, finds their deadlines and answers your questions with artificial intelligence that runs entirely on your phone. This page explains what happens to your data.

In short

  • AuraNote collects no data and shares none.
  • The app has no permission to access the Internet: it cannot send anything.
  • There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Your notes stay on your phone

The text of your notes, their categories, keywords, deadlines and summaries, Aura’s memory (the words and habits it learns from your use) and the photos of the pages you scan are stored in the app’s private storage, on your phone. They are not included in your phone’s Google backup. Nobody else, not even the developer, can access them.

The artificial intelligence runs on the phone

Google’s Gemma 3 1B and EmbeddingGemma models, and the reading of text from scanned pages (Google ML Kit and Tesseract), run on your phone. Your notes are not sent to any server to be analysed. The models are downloaded by Google Play when the app is installed, like the rest of the app.

Google services used on the phone

  • Dictation. When you dictate a note, your voice is handed to Android’s speech recognition service (Google on most phones). AuraNote asks it to work offline. If the offline language pack is not installed, that service may use the network: your voice is then processed by Google, under Google’s privacy policy. AuraNote only receives the recognised text.
  • Document scanner. The scanner is provided by Google Play services, which hold the camera during the scan. Only the pages you confirm come back to AuraNote, which keeps them on the phone.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only for dictation, requested on first use. You can refuse it and type your notes.
  • Foreground service (data sync): used by Google Play to unpack the AI models after they are downloaded.
  • Run at startup, keep awake, network state: to resume pending note analysis and app maintenance. The network state is read, never used to send data.

AuraNote does not ask for access to the camera, your contacts, your location or your files.

Deleting your data

A deleted note goes to the app’s trash, where it is erased for good after 30 days, or at once if you empty the trash; the photos of its pages are erased with it. A scanned page can also be deleted on its own. Uninstalling AuraNote erases all its data from the phone.

Children

AuraNote is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data about anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with its effective date.
