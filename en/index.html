Aura Brain, the brain of AuraNote

Many AI apps send your text to a large model on the Internet, the same one for everyone. Aura Brain does the opposite: it lives on your phone and grows with you.

It learns from you, and nobody else

Your words, your habits, your gestures: repeat a gesture on three notes and it suggests it; accept it three times and it does it on its own, and tells you. Every Brain is unique.

It explains what it does

Why this note is a task, why these two notes are linked: it tells you in one sentence.

It never makes things up

Its answers are built from your notes. The AI model is a tool it uses, checks, and can do without.

It adapts to your phone

It decides how much to rely on the AI depending on your phone; on a modest phone, it works with its rules alone.

Take notes your way

Type, speak or scan a page: Aura does the rest.

Type or speak

Dictation in English, French or Arabic, offline when the language pack is installed.

Scan a page

The scanner frames the page, reads printed text, Arabic included, and keeps the photo with the note.

Aura sorts for you

Title, category, keywords and deadline: “tomorrow”, “Friday”, “November 3” become a date.

Ask Aura

Aura answers from your notes, shows the ones it read, and never makes things up.

Your questions

“What haven’t I finished?”, “Where is the kitchen project at?”, “What’s overdue?”

Linked notes

Notes about the same subject are linked together, and you can see why.

Your deadlines

“What’s due tomorrow?”: Aura finds the deadlines in all your notes, and what is overdue.

Your privacy first

AuraNote’s artificial intelligence runs on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere: the app doesn’t even have permission to access the Internet.

  • No Internet permission: the app cannot send anything.
  • The AI (Google’s Gemma 3) and the reading of scanned pages run on the phone.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • Your notes stay out of the Google backup.
  • Everything works in airplane mode.

Three languages

Interface in English, French and Arabic, right to left, and notes in the language of your choice: Aura answers in the language of the question.

Frequently asked questions

Which phone do I need?

Android 8 or later. The AI needs a phone with 4 GB of RAM; on a more modest phone, AuraNote sorts your notes with its rules alone.

How much space does AuraNote take?

About 1 GB for the AI models, downloaded by Google Play with the app.

Are my notes backed up?

No: they stay on your phone and nowhere else. Uninstalling the app erases them.

Does the scanner read handwriting?

No, only printed text. In Arabic, reading a page takes about fifteen seconds.

How much does AuraNote cost?

Nothing. AuraNote is free and has no ads.
